The eye behind the lens.

I’m Christian Koppel — a skateboarding cinematographer turned award-winning luxury wedding filmmaker. I don’t stage moments; I hunt them. My films are built from the glances, the laughter, and the quiet seconds in between.

Method

The three pillars.

I.

Unposed, always

No direction, no staging, no re-takes. The day unfolds; the camera simply pays attention.

II.

Cinema-grade craft

Every frame is exposed, graded, and cut to the standard of the films we grew up loving.

III.

Invisible on the day

You will forget we are there. That is the point — presence without intrusion.
